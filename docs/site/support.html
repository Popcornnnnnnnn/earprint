Frequently Asked Questions

Why are no headphones showing up yet?

1. Open iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Earprint (or Health app → Profile → Apps → Earprint) and make sure Headphone Audio Levels is enabled.
2. iOS writes Headphone Audio Levels only while audio is actively playing through headphones — wearing headphones with silent ANC active does not generate samples in Apple Health.

Which headphones get 3D collectible cards?

AirPods (1st–4th gen, ANC & standard), AirPods Pro (1–3), and AirPods Max (Lightning & USB-C) get matched collectible artwork and interactive 3D models. Beats and other Bluetooth headphones still count toward your Rings and total listening stats.

What are the 6 collector card tiers?

Each headphone card levels up automatically by lifetime listening hours: Bronze Slate (0h+), Silver Aluminum (25h+), Fjord Teal (120h+), Deep Purple (400h+), Midnight Carbon (1,000h+), and Liquid Holo (2,000h+).

Does Earprint upload my Health data?

No. Everything stays 100% on your iPhone with zero servers, accounts, or analytics. See our Privacy Policy.

常见问题（简体中文）

为什么页面里还没有出现耳机卡片？

1. 请打开 iPhone 设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 健康 → Earprint，确认已开启「耳机音量」读取权限。
2. iOS 仅在耳机播放音频时记录音量数据；纯佩戴降噪但不播放声音时，Apple「健康」不会写入曝光记录。

哪些耳机支持生成 3D 收藏卡？

AirPods（第 1–4 代含降噪版）、AirPods Pro（第 1–3 代）与 AirPods Max（Lightning 与 USB-C）均支持收藏卡片与 3D 立体模型；其他蓝牙耳机也会完整计入「聆听环」总时长与声纹统计。

6 阶卡片等级是多少小时解锁？

累计聆听时长达到门槛自动晋级：青铜岩板（0h+）、银铝（25h+）、峡湾青（120h+）、深紫釉（400h+）、午夜碳纤（1,000h+）、液态全息（2,000h+）。

我的健康数据会上传吗？

不会。全部统计与导出均在你的 iPhone 本地完成。详情请查看 隐私政策，或写信联系 wmax43906@gmail.com。
