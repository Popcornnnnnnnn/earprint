Frequently Asked Questions

Why are no headphones showing up yet?

1. Open iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Earprint (or Health app → Profile → Apps → Earprint) and make sure Headphone Audio Levels is enabled.
2. iOS writes Headphone Audio Levels only while audio is actively playing through headphones — wearing headphones with silent ANC active does not generate samples in Apple Health.

Which headphones get interactive 3D cards?

In v1.1, all Bluetooth headphones with listening data get interactive 3D collectible cards. Unrecognized over-ear models use HeadphoneGeneric.usdz, and unrecognized earbuds use a procedurally generated model. Recognized models also offer their retail finishes.

What are the 6 collector card tiers?

Each headphone card levels up automatically by lifetime listening hours: Bronze Slate (0h+), Silver Aluminum (25h+), Fjord Teal (120h+), Deep Purple (400h+), Midnight Carbon (1,000h+), and Liquid Holo (2,000h+).

Is Earprint free?

Yes. Earprint v1.1 is 100% free with no ads, subscriptions, or paywalls — including 3D cards, four custom foils, slab engraving, 2026 Wrapped reels, multi-year exports, and Live Listening Sessions.

How does Live Listening Session work?

Start or stop a headphone listening session manually in Settings (or automatically when connected headphones match a card), and view recent loudness and the rest countdown on Dynamic Island or the Lock Screen. Tap the Live Activity to open that device card, or use Apple Shortcuts to start the session, reset the rest timer, or stop it.

Does Earprint upload my Health data?

No. Everything stays 100% on your iPhone with zero servers, accounts, or analytics. See our Privacy Policy.

简体中文 · 常见问题

常见问题（简体中文）

为什么页面里还没有出现耳机卡片？

1. 请打开 iPhone 设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 健康 → Earprint，确认已开启「耳机音量」读取权限。
2. iOS 仅在耳机播放音频时记录音量数据；纯佩戴降噪但不播放声音时，Apple「健康」不会写入曝光记录。

哪些耳机支持可交互的 3D 收藏卡？

在 v1.1 中，所有有聆听记录的蓝牙耳机都能生成可交互的 3D 收藏卡。未识别的头戴式耳机使用 HeadphoneGeneric.usdz，未识别的耳塞使用程序生成的模型。可识别的型号还支持对应的市售配色。

6 阶卡片等级是多少小时解锁？

累计聆听时长达到门槛自动晋级：青铜岩（0h+）、银灰铝（25h+）、峡湾青（120h+）、深紫（400h+）、午夜碳纤（1,000h+）、流光全息（2,000h+）。

Earprint 是免费的吗？

是的。Earprint v1.1 全部功能 100% 免费开放，无广告、无订阅、无付费墙——包含 3D 耳机卡、四款金属卡面、自定义刻字、2026 年度回顾短片、跨年记录导出与实时聆听会话。

实时聆听会话怎么用？

可在设置中手动开启或结束耳机聆听会话（佩戴已建卡耳机时也会自动同步），并在灵动岛或锁屏查看近期音量与休息倒计时；轻点实时活动可打开对应耳机卡片。通过 Apple「快捷指令」可执行开始会话、重置休息计时或结束会话。

我的健康数据会上传吗？

不会。全部统计与导出均在你的 iPhone 本地完成。详情请查看 隐私政策，或写信联系 wmax43906@gmail.com。
