Quick Answers

Why are no headphones showing up yet?

Ensure Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Earprint has Headphone Audio Levels enabled. Note iOS records samples only while audio actively plays — silent ANC does not generate Health samples.

Which headphones receive interactive 3D cards?

AirPods (1st–4th gen, ANC & standard), AirPods Pro (1–3), and AirPods Max (Lightning & USB-C) receive matched collectible artwork and 3D models. Beats and third-party Bluetooth headphones still count toward Rings and totals.

How do the 6 collector tiers unlock?

Cards upgrade automatically by lifetime listening hours: Bronze Slate (0h+), Silver Aluminum (25h+), Fjord Teal (120h+), Deep Purple (400h+), Midnight Carbon (1,000h+), and Liquid Holo (2,000h+).

Is Earprint affiliated with Apple or a medical device?

No. Earprint is an independent app by Weizhi Wang and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Apple Inc. Acoustic metrics and WHO budgets are informational only and not a medical device.
