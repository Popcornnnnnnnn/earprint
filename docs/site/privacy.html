English Privacy Policy

1. Overview & Data Not Collected

Earprint does not collect, transmit, store on external servers, sell, or share any personal or Health data. For Apple App Store Connect Privacy Nutrition Label purposes, Earprint operates under Data Not Collected.

2. Apple Health (HealthKit) Access (Read-Only)

With your explicit permission, Earprint requests read-only access to a single HealthKit data type: Headphone Audio Levels (HKQuantityType(.headphoneAudioExposure)), including sample start/end timestamps, A-weighted sound level (dB(A)), and associated HKDevice / HKSource hardware metadata.

  • Release builds request read-only Apple Health access (toShare: []) and never write data to Apple Health (NSHealthUpdateUsageDescription is present in Info.plist solely to satisfy App Store Connect validation).
  • Earprint never accesses heart rate, workouts, sleep, medical records, microphone, camera, contacts, location, or media playback names.

3. On-Device Processing & Storage

  • Local Cache: Compact derived totals and an incremental sync anchor are stored locally inside Application Support (listening-aggregate.json) under iOS file protection (NSFileProtectionCompleteUntilFirstUserAuthentication).
  • App Group for Widgets: Derived summaries are written to the private local App Group container (group.com.popcornnn.earprint) solely to power Earprint Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets on your iPhone.
  • User-Initiated Exports: Shared poster PNGs are rendered on-device and leave your phone only when you choose a destination via the iOS Share Sheet.

4. Zero Servers, Zero Tracking, HealthKit Compliance

Earprint uses no backend servers, accounts, crash reporting SDKs, analytics, or advertising frameworks. Consistent with Apple App Review Guidelines Section 5.1.3, HealthKit data is never used for advertising or disclosed to third parties.

5. Managing Permission & Deleting Data

Revoke HealthKit access anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Earprint. Uninstalling Earprint removes all local caches; your original Apple Health history is never modified.

6. Disclaimers & Contact

Earprint visualizations and WHO/ITU H.870 weekly dose estimates are informational only and are not a medical device. Earprint is an independent app by Weizhi Wang and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Apple Inc.

简体中文隐私政策

一、概述（不收集数据）

Earprint 不会收集、上传、在外部服务器存储、出售或向任何第三方共享你的个人信息或健康数据。在 App Store「App 隐私」分类中，本应用属于不收集数据（Data Not Collected）。

二、访问的 Apple「健康」（HealthKit）范围（严格只读）

仅在获得你的明确授权后，Earprint 会以只读方式读取唯一一项数据类型：耳机音量（Headphone Audio Levels）（HKQuantityType(.headphoneAudioExposure)），包括起止时间、A 计权分贝值（dB(A)）以及关联耳机设备的系统元信息（HKDevice 与 HKSource）。

  • 正式发布版本仅请求 Apple「健康」只读权限（toShare: []），不会向 Apple「健康」写入任何数据（Info.plist 中的 NSHealthUpdateUsageDescription 仅用于满足 App Store Connect 静态校验要求）。
  • 不访问心率、体能训练、睡眠、医疗记录、麦克风、相机、通讯录、位置或具体的播放歌名内容。

三、本地处理与存储方式

  • 本地加密缓存： 所有采样在设备本地聚合后保存于应用沙盒 Application Support（listening-aggregate.json），受 iOS 系统文件加密保护。
  • 桌面与锁屏小组件共享容器： 仅将汇总展示数据本地写入 group.com.popcornnn.earprint 容器，仅供主应用与内置小组件扩展使用，绝不离开本机。
  • 主动导出图片： 生成的分享海报在本地渲染，仅在你主动通过系统分享面板发送时才分享给你选择的目标。

四、零服务器、零追踪与合规承诺

无账号、无后台服务器、无任何第三方统计或广告 SDK。依据 Apple《App 审核指南》第 5.1.3 条，所有 HealthKit 数据绝不用于广告营销或向第三方披露。

五、管理权限与清除数据

可随时前往 iPhone 设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 健康 → Earprint 关闭读取权限；卸载应用即永久删除本地缓存，不会修改你的 Apple「健康」原始数据。
