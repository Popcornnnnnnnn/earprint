## App Review Notes (v1.1.0)

Paste into **App Store Connect → App Review Information → Notes**.

```text
Earprint v1.1.0 (build 10)

1. Read-only HealthKit access
Earprint reads one data type, Headphone Audio Levels (HKQuantityType(.headphoneAudioExposure)), using toShare: []. The Release build excludes the DEBUG-only HealthKit seeder. The Release binary check (nm -u) requires zero HealthKit write, save, or delete symbols. NSHealthUpdateUsageDescription is included for validation and describes the app’s read-only behavior. Processing stays on the iPhone. No account or sign-in is required.

2. Clean-device and denied-permission testing
The first-run welcome screen explains Health access. With zero readable samples, Collection and Rings show empty states with a Sync Apple Health action and no fabricated metrics. Reviewers can browse Settings, Model Gallery, Card Tiers, and Credits & Attributions without Health data. To populate a card, play audio through headphones until iOS records a Headphone Audio Levels sample in Health, then tap Sync Apple Health. Manually added entries attributed to the iPhone itself are excluded.

3. StoreKit 2 purchases and restore
Open Collection → Settings (gear) → Earprint Pro. Earprint Pro is a one-time non-consumable purchase. The Tip Jar in Settings offers three optional consumables that grant no Pro access.

Grandfathering grants free lifetime Pro only when a verified AppTransaction receipt has environment .production and originalAppVersion parses as an integer build below 10. Sandbox, TestFlight, and Xcode transactions never qualify, so a fresh sandbox account in App Review can test the purchase and restore flows without inherited Pro access.

Tap the purchase button to unlock Pro. Confirm that a Pro feature, such as Export Archive in Settings, becomes available. To test restore, use the same account after reinstalling or on another device and tap Restore Purchases in Earprint Pro. If StoreKit has already redelivered the entitlement, Restore Purchases should keep Pro unlocked. Tips are repeatable consumables and are not restored.

4. Live Listening Session and Shortcuts
With Pro unlocked, go to Settings → Earprint Pro → Live Listening Session to start manually. The same control becomes Stop Listening Session while active. The session displays a rest countdown and available loudness information on the Lock Screen and on Dynamic Island on supported iPhones. In Shortcuts, add the Earprint AppIntents actions Start Listening Session, Reset Listening Rest Timer, and Stop Listening Session to test those controls. Reset restarts the break countdown for the current session.

5. Disclaimers
Earprint is independently developed by Weizhi Wang and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Apple Inc. Listening summaries and WHO/ITU exposure references are for personal information only. Earprint is not a medical device or a diagnostic tool.
```
