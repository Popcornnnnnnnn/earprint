Earprint — 7 UI directions

Every mockup uses the same 4 weeks of sample listening data, so you only compare the look and feel. Each direction has an in-app screen and a share card. Pick one (or mix, e.g. "B + G"). A–F are main styles. G is an add-on that works with any of them.

A

Rings (current sample)

Dark, glowing, a little sci-fi. Your listening becomes a unique "fingerprint": each ring is a day, each segment an hour, color is loudness.

  • Strong signature visual — no one else has it
  • Feels premium and Apple-like

Ink Ribbon (editorial)

Warm paper, serif type, and one flowing ink waveform of your week, like a magazine page. Calm and "文艺". It fits Xiaohongshu's taste very well.

  • Very easy to read — one wave = one week
  • Looks great as a photo post; light mode stands out in a dark feed
GOOD FORXiaohongshu users, young women, journaling fans
RISKLess "techy"; needs strong typography to feel premium

C

Headphone Collection

Every pair of headphones you've owned becomes a collectible card. More hours = rarer card (Bronze → Silver → Holo). Uses the device name and model that Health stores.

  • Fun for people with several AirPods / Beats
  • "Lifetime hours" and "first met" dates create emotion
GOOD FORGadget collectors, digital bloggers (数码博主)
RISKWeak for people with only one pair

Ear Buddy (cute pet)

A small character wears your headphones. Its mood follows your weekly hearing budget: happy when you listen safely, sleepy when you overdo it. Gentle health nudges instead of warnings.

  • Emotional and very "可爱" — strong for XHS / Douyin
  • Turns hearing health into a daily habit, good for retention
GOOD FORStudents, young women, casual users
RISKNeeds a real illustrator for the character; can feel childish

E

Listening Receipt

Your week printed as a shop receipt, with line items, totals and a barcode. A proven viral meme format (like "Receiptify" for Spotify).

  • Instantly understood, funny, very shareable
  • Cheap to build; easy to make monthly / yearly versions
GOOD FORMeme sharing, comment-section posts
RISKNovelty fades; better as a share format than the whole app

  • Big emotional moment — people love "my year in…"
  • Every slide is a ready-made post
GOOD FORMonthly / year-end campaigns (Dec 2026 is close!)
